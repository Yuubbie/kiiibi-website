Meet the founder

Adewumi UbongFounder and Chairman, Kweevah Bath Body Naturals

She is a financial management professional with over 9 years of hands-on experience in financial oversight and IFRS compliance. As a Qualified by Experience (QBE) accountant, she has a complete understanding of the entire accounting process, from leading complex audits for diverse organizations to collaborating on high-stakes joint projects with Big Four firms. She specializes in regularizing accounts, identifying systemic discrepancies, and strengthening internal controls to ensure total financial transparency, the same rigor she has brought to building KIIIBI.

That discipline shows up in the product: KIIIBI is shea butter and body care made the way it should be, whipped and cured with care, no fillers, no shortcuts, made in Nigeria for every season, Harmattan included.
