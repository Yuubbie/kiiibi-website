About KIIIBI

Made by people, not a factory line.

KIIIBI started with one belief: skin does better on fewer, better ingredients. Everything we make begins as raw shea butter and stays that way. Whipped, cured, and jarred in small batches by hand.

  • No fillers or diluents. What we whip is what we jar.
  • Cruelty free, never tested on animals at any stage.
  • Formulated and worn through Nigeria's driest months.
