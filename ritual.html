The Ritual

From nut to jar, nothing is rushed.

Every batch of KIIIBI starts as a raw shea nut and ends as a jar on your shelf. This is the process, unchanged since the first batch.

01

Raw Shea Nuts

Sourced from shea trees across West Africa's savannah belt, selected at peak oil content.

02

Cracked & Roasted

Nuts are cracked, sorted, and slow-roasted to unlock the butter's natural richness.

03

Milled & Processed

Cleaned, sorted, and milled into a raw paste, with no industrial solvents, ever.

04

Whipped to Butter

Whipped until it turns pale, airy, and smooth: real shea butter, done properly.

05

Poured & Cured

Poured into jars, cured, and packaged before it ever leaves our facility.

KIIIBI is the shea butter and body care line of Kweevah Bath Body Naturals, whipped and made in Nigeria for every season, Harmattan included.
